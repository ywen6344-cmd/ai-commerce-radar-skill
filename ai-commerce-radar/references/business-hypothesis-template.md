## Starter Hypotheses

### H001: AI Product Visibility Optimization

One-line hypothesis: sellers need to make product pages, catalogs, reviews, attributes, and FAQs easier for AI shopping assistants to understand, compare, and recommend.

Target users: Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, and DTC sellers.

Triggering signals:

- Amazon Alexa for Shopping moves AI into the shopping search and purchase flow.
- Shopify Global Catalog MCP makes agent-facing catalog discovery explicit.
- ChatGPT, Gemini, YouTube, and Google Ads are pushing product discovery and agentic commerce surfaces.

MVP:

```text
Input: product URL or catalog export
Output: AI-ready title, attributes, FAQ, comparison points, review summary, structured feed suggestions
```

Validation: manually audit 10 seller product pages and charge for an optimization report.

### H002: TikTok Shop Compliance And Metrics Radar

One-line hypothesis: TikTok Shop sellers need plain-language, category-specific action checklists for policy changes and seller metrics.

Target users: TikTok Shop US sellers, agencies, and operators.

Triggering signals:

- Frequent TikTok Shop Policy Pulse updates.
- VoC, ISRR, AHR, SPS, documentation requirements, return cost responsibility, and creator restrictions are becoming more complex.

MVP:

```text
Weekly policy scan + affected category checklist + risk score + recommended actions
```

Validation: run a weekly email/WeChat group for 20 sellers and test consulting or subscription demand.

### H003: Shopify Agent-Ready Catalog Checker

One-line hypothesis: Shopify merchants will need catalog hygiene and structured product data to perform well in AI-agent product discovery.

Target users: Shopify DTC brands, Shopify app developers, catalog/feed managers.

Triggering signals:

- Shopify Global Catalog MCP and Storefront Catalog MCP expose products to agents.
- UCP/MCP commerce infrastructure is becoming a practical integration layer.

MVP:

```text
Check product catalog completeness, variant clarity, media quality, region availability, returns/shipping data, and AI-search readability.
```

Validation: package as a one-time audit for Shopify stores.

### H004: Commerce Media Creative Automation

One-line hypothesis: cross-border sellers need cheaper generation and testing of creator-style, platform-specific ad creatives across YouTube, TikTok, Meta, and Amazon.

Target users: cross-border sellers, agencies, small DTC teams.

Triggering signals:

- YouTube Brandcast introduced AI-assisted sponsorships and multimodal video creation.
- Platforms are connecting creator content, affiliate links, CTV, and checkout.

MVP:

```text
Input: product page + target platform + audience
Output: creator brief, short video scripts, ad angles, thumbnail ideas, compliance warnings
```

Validation: deliver manual creative packs to 5 sellers and measure repeat purchase.
