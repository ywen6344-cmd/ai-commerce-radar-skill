# Scoring Rules

## Source Grades

S: official original source  
Policy files, platform announcements, API changelogs, docs, investor releases, SEC filings, regulator pages, court/customs/tax documents.

A: direct actor  
Company executives, product leads, maintainers, seller dashboard notices, official event pages, platform employee statements.

B: high-quality secondary source  
Research firms, vertical media, professional analysts, reputable news articles, industry reports.

C: social or community lead  
X/Twitter, Reddit, forums, newsletters, group chats, short videos, reposts, anonymous screenshots.

C-grade information can trigger investigation, but should not become a conclusion unless traced back to S/A evidence.

## 100-Point Score

Relevance: 0-25

- 25: directly affects AI, technology, cross-border commerce, platform rules, distribution, compliance, or business opportunity
- 15: adjacent but potentially useful
- 5: broad tech/business news with weak fit

Source credibility: 0-25

- S = 25
- A = 20
- B = 12
- C = 5

Freshness: 0-20

- 24 hours = 20
- 7 days = 15
- 30 days = 8
- Older = 3, unless it is a still-active deadline or enforcement date

Business impact: 0-20

- 20: changes cost, traffic, compliance, fulfillment, conversion, ads, catalog, checkout, or automation potential
- 10: affects research narrative but has unclear business action
- 3: interesting but hard to act on

Verifiability: 0-10

- 10: original link plus version/date plus corroborating source or direct documentation
- 6: original link exists but market impact is not yet visible
- 2: indirect report only

## Entry Thresholds

- 90-100: urgent alert and business hypothesis candidate
- 80-89: daily brief
- 60-79: store for weekly review
- Under 60: ignore or keep only as a lead

## Verification Labels

已验证: at least one S/A source plus independent corroboration, direct documentation, or observable product/policy behavior.

交叉验证中: an S/A source exists, but execution, adoption, enforcement, or market effect is not yet clear.

待验证: only B/C sources, previews, rumors, or unconfirmed reports.

## Triangulation Patterns

Policy change:

```text
Official policy text + platform enforcement announcement + seller/community operational feedback
```

AI product change:

```text
Official release note + API/doc page + developer test or customer-facing product behavior
```

Platform rule change:

```text
Platform announcement + dashboard/community evidence + rule/fee/API page update
```

Market opportunity:

```text
Search/category trend + product/ad/listing evidence + seller pain, hiring, funding, or tool adoption
```

## Rejection Rules

Reject items that:

- Have no original source and no clear path to verification
- Are broad hype without impact on rules, costs, workflows, traffic, compliance, or buyer behavior
- Duplicate another stronger signal
- Cannot produce an industry judgment, business implication, or next action
- Depend on social chatter without S/A support
