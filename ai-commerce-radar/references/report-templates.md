## Tone And Length

- Daily brief: 5-10 signals, under 1,200 Chinese characters when possible.
- Weekly review: 5-8 major items, business implications first.
- Urgent alert: short and actionable.
- Always include source links.
- Do not over-explain generic AI or e-commerce concepts.
- Use Chinese by default when the user speaks Chinese.
